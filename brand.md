## Typography — Familjen Grotesk + Charis SIL

- **Display + interface:** Familjen Grotesk (500, tight tracking −0.035em for headlines, as in the reference)
- **Every Hausa, Yoruba and Igbo word:** Charis SIL. Made by SIL for languages with stacked tone marks and hooked letters. Tested: renders ẹ́ ọ̀ ṣ ụ ị ṅ ɓ ɗ ƙ correctly. Gentium Book Plus and Bricolage Grotesque were rejected for misplacing or dropping marks.
- No mono font: numbers use `tabular-nums`.

Wired via `next/font/google` in `src/app/layout.tsx` (subsets latin, latin-ext, vietnamese). CSS variables `--font-sans`, `--font-serif`. Class `.word` for any language text, `.display` for headlines.

### Type scale

Perfect fourth (1.333), fluid only on display sizes: `--step--1` 0.8125rem · `--step-0` 1rem · `--step-1` 1.333rem · `--step-2` to `--step-5` clamp up to 4.2rem.

## Gradients (not used)

The reference is flat colour. Texture comes from the halftone image (`public/images/brand/ijapa-halftone.webp`, square dots in cream on `#82221E`), not gradients.

## Tone and voice

### Words to use
Plain, exact and proud. Name things: "reviewed", "AI draft", "recorded by", "dialect". Short sentences. Credit people by name. Say what the AI got wrong, without hype.

### Words to avoid
"Revolutionary", "unlock", "seamless", "leverage", "empower", "Africa's first". No exoticising ("exotic tongues", "ancient mysteries"). No apology for the languages.

### Voice example
> Words that last. Voices that endure. Every name here was checked by someone who grew up speaking it.

## Usage dos and don'ts

- **Do** keep one clay-red field per page (the hero). Other bands use ink or paper.
- **Do** set every language word in Charis SIL with `.word`. Never in the grotesk.
- **Do** use halftone treatments of real, licensed photos for brand imagery, with credit.
- **Don't** use ochre for text or large areas; it's a marker colour.
- **Don't** add purple/blue AI gradients, glassmorphism, or ankara/kente pattern wallpaper.
- **Don't** shrink tap targets below 44px.
