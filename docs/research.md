# Research notes (Oct 2026)

## Competitors and existing resources

- **Kamus.com.ng**: English–Hausa dictionary since 2014, example sentences, Hausa plurals, word origins. English-first, no tones or native audio.
- **HausaDictionary.com**: wiki-style. Several Play Store dictionary apps too.
- **UNICCON**: sells Hausa/Yoruba speech recognition, text-to-speech and translation APIs.
- **N-ATLAS** (NCAIR + Awarri): government open-source model on Llama-3 8B, covers Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Nigerian English. Lives on Hugging Face (NCAIR1/N-ATLaS), not a consumer product. Products over 1,000 users need a formal agreement.
- **National AI Innovation Challenge**: rewards tools, datasets and voice-first apps built on N-ATLAS. Check if still open.
- **HausaNLP catalog** (catalog.hausanlp.org): datasets, tools, papers. Researchers say Hausa still lacks open datasets; most corpora come from BBC/VOA news and religious texts.
- **CLEAR Global**: open Hausa TTS/ASR models and synthetic voice data on Hugging Face.

Gap: nobody owns deep, structured, human-checked Hausa with tones, dialects and native audio.

## Free image sources

- Wikimedia Commons (use CC0 / CC-BY only, credit CC-BY)
- Openverse (openverse.org), license filter
- iNaturalist (filter out CC-BY-NC)
- Smithsonian Open Access, Met Museum open collection
- Unsplash / Pexels / Pixabay: fine for illustration, but terms bar building a competing collection

Paid stock (Shutterstock ~$29–49/month for 10 images, Adobe Stock ~$30/month for 10) is a poor fit: little Hausa content, licenses usually block API redistribution and AI training.

Plan: open sources for animals/plants, shoot cultural items yourself at markets, pay local photographers per photo with written rights transfer.

## Funding

- **Lacuna Fund**: open datasets for African languages. Past budgets $10k–100k, up to $200k. Lead applicant must be an organization based in the country. Register with CAC first.
- **Wikimedia Rapid Fund**: $500–5,000, 1–12 month projects. Work must improve Wikimedia projects (open licenses).
- **British Library Endangered Archives Programme**: manuscript digitization. EAP535 already put ~695 Hausa Ajami items online.
- **N-ATLAS challenge**: cash plus ecosystem integration.

Non-cash: credit, leaderboards, partnerships with Hausa departments at BUK, ABU, UDUS.
